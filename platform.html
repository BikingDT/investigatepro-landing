The method

Four ICAM levels. None skipped.

01

People

Actions and conditions at the point of work — captured without blame.

02

Task & environment

The job as done, the conditions it was done in, and where they diverged from the plan.

03

Organisation

The factors that set the conditions — prompted on every case, not just the big ones.

04

Defences

Absent or failed defences, assessed against the hierarchy of controls.

The coach

Guidance that reads the case, not a script.

Evidence gaps, witness questions, unexamined levels, drafted factors — each suggestion carries its source from inside the case, and nothing is filed until an investigator accepts it. The report goes out attributed to the lead investigator.
