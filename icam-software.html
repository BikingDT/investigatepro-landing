Templates, EHS suites or dedicated ICAM software?

There are three broad ways to run ICAM, and each fits a different organisation.

Three ways to run ICAM investigations compared
ApproachWhere it fitsWhere it struggles
Word or Excel templatesOne trained investigator and a few cases a year. Free to start: see our ICAM template.Quality depends on whoever fills it in. Evidence and actions live in separate files, and nothing gets checked before sign-off.
General EHS platformsLarge operators that need one register for incidents, audits, permits and compliance.The investigation module is usually a form. The method lives in the investigator’s head, not in the software.
Dedicated ICAM softwareTeams that want one method on every case, run by every supervisor, with output that feeds the system of record.It is a second tool next to your register, so the hand-over between the two needs agreeing up front.

If you run 5,000 people and want one suite for everything, a general EHS platform is the better buy. InvestigatePro is built for getting one investigation done properly this week, at a business that does not have a full-time investigations team.

Questions about ICAM software.

More on the full FAQ and the pricing page. New to the method? Read the ICAM methodology guide.

What is ICAM software?

Software that runs the Incident Cause Analysis Method end to end: gathering evidence, building the sequence of events, analysing contributing factors across ICAM’s four levels, and turning findings into actions and a report. Good ICAM software enforces the method. A blank form with ICAM headings does not.

Does InvestigatePro replace our EHS platform?

No. Your EHS platform stays your system of record. InvestigatePro is where the investigation itself is done, and its report and actions feed into that platform.

Does the AI decide the causes?

No. The coach prompts for evidence gaps, witness questions and empty ICAM levels, and drafts factors tied to their source. It cannot suggest a cause without a record behind it, and nothing is filed until an investigator accepts it. Starter has no coach at all.

How is ICAM different from a 5 Whys or root-cause analysis?

ICAM assumes an incident has several contributing causes across the system, not a single one. It looks beyond individual actions to failed defences, task and environmental conditions, and organisational factors. A 5 Whys follows a single chain. Compare methods in ICAM vs SCAT.

Does it suit mining contractors?

Yes. That is the core use: drilling, blasting, geotech, ventilation, plant hire and mine-engineering contractors who must investigate to their principal’s standard without an investigations department of their own. See mining incident investigation.

Does it work for US MSHA and OSHA reporting?

The investigation method is the same wherever you operate, and the report supports what you file. The statutory deadlines still apply: MSHA notification within 15 minutes (30 CFR 50.10) and form 7000-1 within 10 working days, and OSHA within 8 hours for a fatality or 24 hours for hospitalisation, amputation or eye loss (29 CFR 1904.39).
