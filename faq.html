The method

What is ICAM?

ICAM, the Incident Cause Analysis Method, is a systematic way to investigate workplace incidents. It examines four areas: absent or failed defences, individual or team actions, task or environmental conditions, and organisational factors. It shows what happened, why it happened, and what would stop it happening again.

What is PEEPO?

PEEPO sorts evidence into People, Environment, Equipment, Procedures and Organisation, so collection covers the systemic as well as the obvious.

Does it replace our EHS platform?

No. Your EHS platform stays your system of record. InvestigatePro is where the investigation gets done properly, and its report and actions feed that system.

The coach

What does the coach do?

It reads the case in front of it: it flags evidence gaps, suggests witness questions, prompts ICAM levels left empty, and drafts factors, each tied to its source inside the case.

Does the coach decide the causes?

No. It prompts and drafts; the investigator decides. Nothing is filed until an investigator accepts it, and the report is attributed to the lead investigator.

Is our investigation data used to train AI models?

No. Your investigation data is not used to train AI models. Verify against provider terms
