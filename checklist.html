🔍 ICAM Incident Investigation Checklist

25 Essential Steps for Thorough Root Cause Analysis

1 Initial Response (First 30 Minutes)

Secure the scene — Prevent further harm and preserve evidence
Ensure medical attention — Confirm all injured persons are receiving care
Notify relevant parties — Supervisors, safety team, management as required
Document initial conditions — Photos, videos, sketches before anything changes
Identify witnesses — List everyone present before memories fade
💡 Pro Tip:

Take photos from multiple angles and distances. What seems obvious now may be crucial later.

2 Evidence Collection

Collect physical evidence — Equipment, materials, PPE, environmental samples
Gather documentation — Work orders, procedures, training records, maintenance logs
Review CCTV/recordings — Security cameras, dashcams, wearable devices
Download equipment data — Black boxes, telemetry, sensor logs
Map the scene — Create detailed diagram with measurements

3 Witness Interviews

Interview separately — Prevent influence between witnesses
Use open questions — "Tell me what happened" not "Did X cause this?"
Document verbatim quotes — Exact words matter for accuracy
Explore the timeline — Build minute-by-minute sequence of events
Ask about "normal" operations — Understand if this was typical or different
💡 Pro Tip:

Never ask "Why did you...?" — it triggers defensiveness. Ask "What was happening when...?" instead.

4 ICAM Analysis

Identify Absent/Failed Defences — What barriers should have prevented harm?
List Individual/Team Actions — Actions and decisions (without blame)
Identify Task/Environmental Conditions — Context that shaped decisions
Uncover Organisational Factors — Systems, culture, resources that contributed
Apply the 5 Whys technique — Dig deeper on each contributing factor

5 Corrective Actions & Close-out

Develop SMART actions — Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, Time-bound
Use the hierarchy of controls — Elimination > Substitution > Engineering > Admin > PPE
Assign clear ownership — One person accountable per action item
Share learnings widely — Safety alerts, toolbox talks, cross-site communication
Verify effectiveness — Follow up to confirm actions prevent recurrence
💡 Pro Tip:

60% of incidents have organizational root causes. If all your actions are "retraining," dig deeper.
