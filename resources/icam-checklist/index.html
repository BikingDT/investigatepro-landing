The 25-point ICAM investigation checklist.

Every step of a defensible ICAM investigation — from securing the scene to verifying the actions — in the order a real case runs. Distilled from 15+ years of frontline investigations.

01–05

Immediate response — scene, people, notifications, preservation.

06–12

Evidence — positions, conditions, documents, witnesses.

13–20

Analysis — the four ICAM levels, defences, contributing factors.

21–25

Findings — actions, verification, report and sign-off.
