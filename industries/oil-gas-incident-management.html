Built for the hazards of oil and gas.

The method is the same on every case. What the investigation has to look at depends on the hazard.

Oil and gas hazards, the events they produce, and where the investigation reaches
HazardThe eventsWhere the analysis reaches
Well controlKick-detection failures, blowout-preventer faults and pressure-control events.Well programme design, competency verification and real-time monitoring.
Fire, explosion and hydrocarbon releaseLoss of containment, ignition and uncontrolled releases.Mechanical integrity, management of change and hot-work permits.
Chemical and toxic exposureH₂S exposure, chemical spills and inhalation.Atmospheric monitoring, emergency response and muster.
Isolation and maintenanceUnexpected releases of energy during maintenance.Isolation management, permit to work and verification of isolation.
Lifting and riggingDropped objects, crane and rigging failures on decks and rig floors.Lift planning, equipment inspection and banksman competency.
Marine and divingVessel incidents, man overboard and dive operations.Pre-job risk assessment, communications and emergency preparedness.
Road transportVehicle incidents on the way to and between sites.Journey management, fatigue management and vehicle condition.
Camps and accommodationFood safety, fire and personal safety in remote camps.Facility management and contractor camp standards.

One investigation, end to end.

  1. Log the event

    Classification, location in your site structure, people involved and the first description.

  2. Build the timeline

    Photos, statements and documents attached to the sequence of events. The coach flags gaps.

  3. Analyse with ICAM

    Absent or failed defences, individual or team actions, task or environmental conditions, organisational factors.

  4. Generate the report

    Evidence, timeline, causal analysis and recommendations in one formatted document.

  5. Take actions to verified

    Owners and due dates stay visible until each action is checked and verified.
