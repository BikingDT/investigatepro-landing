Built for construction’s leading causes of harm.

The method is the same on every case. What the investigation has to look at depends on the hazard.

Construction hazards, the events they produce, and where the investigation reaches
HazardThe eventsWhere the analysis reaches
Falls from heightScaffold failures, edge-protection gaps and ladder incidents.Fall-prevention design, inspection frequency, permit-to-work quality and supervision.
Struck-by and caught-betweenPlant and pedestrian interaction, falling objects and crane lifts.Traffic management plans, exclusion zones, spotters and lift planning.
Electrical contactBuried-service strikes, overhead lines and temporary power.Locating services before digging, isolation and electrical permits.
Excavation and trenchingCollapse, engulfment and spoil-pile instability.Soil assessment, shoring inspection, confined-space entry and atmospheric monitoring.
Hot work and fireWelding, grinding and cutting.Hot-work permits, fire watch and control of combustible material.
Structural and formwork failurePropping failures, formwork collapse and instability during construction.Design review, temporary-works sign-off and inspection.
Manual handlingMusculoskeletal injuries: frequent, and often under-investigated.Task design, rotation and the pressure that bypasses mechanical aids.
Subcontractor managementEvents at the principal-contractor interface.Pre-qualification, induction quality and supervision frequency.

One investigation, end to end.

  1. Log the event

    Classification, location in your site structure, people involved and the first description.

  2. Build the timeline

    Photos, statements and documents attached to the sequence of events. The coach flags gaps.

  3. Analyse with ICAM

    Absent or failed defences, individual or team actions, task or environmental conditions, organisational factors.

  4. Generate the report

    Evidence, timeline, causal analysis and recommendations in one formatted document.

  5. Take actions to verified

    Owners and due dates stay visible until each action is checked and verified.
