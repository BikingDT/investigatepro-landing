Common questions

Is there a free trial?

Yes — Starter and Professional start with a 14-day free trial. Create your workspace at app.investigatepro.app/signup; no card required. Business and Enterprise start with a guided pilot on your own cases, set up the same day.

How does per-organisation billing work?

One subscription covers your organisation up to the tier’s user cap — 5 on Starter, 20 on Professional, 100 on Business. No per-seat charges.

What does the AI coach actually do?

It reads the case in front of it: flags evidence gaps, suggests witness questions, prompts unexamined ICAM levels and drafts factors — each tied to its source. Nothing is filed until an investigator accepts it.

Can we change plans later?

Yes — upgrade or downgrade at any time; changes apply from the next billing period.
