02The problem

Incident investigation is broken.

“We investigate incidents, but the same things keep happening.”

Safety teams spend weeks filling out forms, but rarely find the causes and fix the system. Incidents get logged. Reports get filed. Nothing changes.

  • Forms, not thinkingCurrent tools are glorified forms. They capture what happened, not why.
  • Repeat incidentsThe same events come back when the causes in the system are never found.
  • Weeks to closeInvestigations drag on. Actions get forgotten.
  • Wasted investmentEnterprise tools cost $100K+, but investigation is an afterthought.

04How it works

From incident to insight.

  1. Report

    Capture the event while it is fresh, placed in your own site structure.

  2. Investigate

    ICAM-guided analysis with AI coaching.

  3. Analyse

    Find the contributing factors and the underlying causes.

  4. Prevent

    Track actions to closure and share the learnings.

AI coaching throughout. Get unstuck with suggestions as you work: “Have you considered organisational factors?” The coach helps investigators think deeper, without expensive training. Every suggestion is accepted, edited or rejected by the investigator.
