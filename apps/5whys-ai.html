5-Whys AI.

A coach for the 5 Whys that challenges shallow answers, points out blind spots and takes the analysis past the person to the system. It is being built alongside the platform.

Join the list How it will work

The problem with the 5 Whys today.

The method works when it is done well. Most of the time it is done alone, in a hurry, with nobody to push back.

  • It stops too soonTwo or three whys, and the causes in the system are never reached.
  • Nobody challenges the answersWithout a facilitator, weak answers are accepted.
  • The quality depends on who does itNo common standard across a team.

ProblemA forklift struck warehouse racking and damaged it.

CoachSpeed is a factor. Why was the operator going too fast? Consider production pressure, awareness and the equipment.

How it will work.

In the field or at a desk.

  1. Describe the problem

    What happened, in text, by voice or with photos. A clear problem statement gives a better analysis.

  2. Answer the whys

    For each why, the coach asks follow-up questions and challenges weak answers.

  3. Explore the branches

    Real incidents have several causes. Branch the analysis to capture them all.

  4. Export or send on

    Share the analysis as a PDF, or send it to an InvestigatePro investigation.
