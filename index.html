The coach at work · Case 2026-0418

The expertise sits in the case, not in the room.

Isolation failure during a conveyor gearbox change-out, night shift. Four things the coach did with it — and what the investigator did back.

  1. Evidence gap

    Two of five lock points have no verification record, and the isolation certificate is not attached. Ask for the permit and the padlock register before the scene is released.

  2. Witness prompt

    Ask the fitter: at what point did you believe the drive was dead, and what told you so?

  3. Unexamined ICAM level

    Organisational factors are still empty. Two earlier isolation events on this line cite the same handover step.

  4. AI-suggested factorOrganisational

    Isolation verification relied on one person’s check, with no independent confirmation required on night shift.
