InvestigatePro ("we", "us", or "our") uses cookies and similar tracking technologies on our website investigatepro.app and our application. This Cookie Policy explains what cookies are, how we use them, your choices regarding cookies, and further information about cookies.

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your computer or mobile device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently, provide a better user experience, and give website owners information about how the site is being used.

How to Manage Cookies

Browser Controls

Most web browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings:

  • Chrome: Settings → Privacy and Security → Cookies
  • Firefox: Options → Privacy & Security → Cookies
  • Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Cookies
  • Edge: Settings → Privacy, Search, and Services → Cookies

Impact of Disabling Cookies

If you disable cookies:

  • You may not be able to log in to your account
  • Some features may not work correctly
  • We won't be able to remember your preferences

Do Not Track

We Honor DNT Signals

When "Do Not Track" is enabled in your browser:

  • We do not load analytics scripts
  • We do not set analytics cookies
  • We do not track your behavior across sessions

Cookie Consent

When you first visit our website, we display a cookie consent banner. You can:

  • Accept All: Enable all cookie types
  • Essential Only: Use only required cookies
  • Customize: Choose which types to enable

You can change your cookie preferences at any time by clicking "Cookie Settings" in the website footer.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Cookie Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date at the top.
